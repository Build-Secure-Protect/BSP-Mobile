# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file. The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- The autopilot: the core's PLAN tab keeps a flight plan of up to 16 waypoints (ADD HERE takes your own position, REMOVE drops the last) and a cruise height above the ground; GO flies the construct from a helm aboard the way a driver would (turning toward the next waypoint, thrusting once lined up, climbing or descending to the cruise height with the lift aboard), so every rule of driving holds; a waypoint counts within 4 blocks; at the last one the construct stops, parks itself and the core gives a redstone pulse for two seconds. A driver taking a helm, STOP, or a closed region stops the plan. The plan travels with the core.
- GameTests: the steering (bearings, turning, the thrust cone, half thrust when close, climbing and descending to the cruise height), the plan kept on the core and surviving a reload with its cap of 16 and the lock's rule, and a running plan thrusting toward its waypoint, freeing the construct and stopping when a driver takes the helm.
- `/mobile` for operators and BSP-Core admins: `list` (every construct on the server with its id, name, owner, blocks, tier, place and state), `remove <id>` (sets it down where it stands; `remove <id> delete` removes it with its blocks), `tp <id>`, `limits` (the counts, caps and switches as read), `save <id> <name>` (the construct as a structure file under `config/bsp_mobile/constructs/`), `paste <name>` (sets a saved construct down where you look and assembles it, owned by you, parked) and `files`. Admin actions go to the server log.
- Caps: constructs per server (50) and per player (3), checked when a construct is assembled, in the server config under `limits`.
- GameTests: the per-player cap refuses a second construct, a saved construct pastes back as a working copy with its name and tier, and the command lists a construct and sets it down.
- Parts, counted by number anywhere aboard: the Mobile Engine (more acceleration and top speed), the Mobile Floater (more buoyancy), the Mobile Balloon (lift: enough balloons for the construct's mass and it hovers), the Mobile Wing (a half-slab; lift from speed through the engine's own aerodynamics) and the Mobile Jet Engine (four engines' worth of thrust and lift to climb). Flight: space climbs while there is lift, sprint with space descends, balloons hold the height without input, jets alone sink slowly; a totem aboard caps the height above the ground beneath. Each part has a recipe and a tooltip. The HELM tab lists the parts, the lift against the mass, and the height with its ceiling. Settings in the server config under `parts`.
- GameTests: the flight maths (no lift, part lift, hovering, climbing, descending, the ceiling, jets alone), the parts counted aboard and added up into the helm's command with the totem's cap, and the parts' recipes.
- Where constructs may go: with BSP Region Guard the state flag `mobile-constructs` (allow by default) decides per region, set with `/rg flag <region> mobile-constructs deny`; assembling inside a denying region is refused, and a construct whose core stands in one can only back out (the HELM tab says so); releasing is allowed anywhere. A server can be closed to constructs outright (`regions.closed` in its server config), and a server BSP-Core lists as a no-totem server is closed by default (`regions.closedOnNoTotemServers`).
- GameTests: a closed server refuses assembly; the back-out rule; a region denying the flag refuses assembly until it allows it again (Region Guard runs on the test server).
- Damage: a construct that hits terrain or another construct faster than 6 blocks a second loses blocks at the point of contact, one for every 2 blocks a second over that, up to 8, the nearest blocks first, never the core or a helm; they break and drop as if mined. One impact counts per construct per second. A core destroyed while its construct is assembled breaks the construct up: its blocks are set back into the world where it stands, or it lies where it is as a derelict when there is no room, and its owner is told. Blocks aboard are real blocks, so explosions, weapons and tools break them as anywhere. Settings in the server config under `damage`.
- GameTests: the impact rule, knocking the nearest blocks off at a contact but never the core, and a construct breaking up when its core is destroyed.
- The Mobile Helm, crafted from Iron Ingots round a Compass with a Tetrium Ingot on top: a pedestal with a wheel, facing the way its placer looked. Right-click it aboard an assembled construct to take it (a locked core keeps it for those the lock admits); W and S thrust along its facing, A and D turn, sprint doubles the thrust, sneak steps away. Taking the helm frees the construct; with nobody at a helm it parks itself again a few seconds after it stops. A construct with a helm floats like a boat. A totem aboard caps the top speed. The figures are in the server config under `drive`.
- The core's HELM tab: speed, heading and driver, the helms aboard and who may drive, the anchor, and DROP ANCHOR or WEIGH ANCHOR for those the lock admits.
- GameTests: the drive maths (thrust, the top speed, drag, the turn), the helm refusing an unassembled structure and a locked core and taking its driver, the helm's recipe; an optional test drives a construct, which the headless server cannot do (no physics body is made there), so driving is checked in game.
- The Mobile Core Lock, crafted from Tetrium Ingots, Iron Ingots and a Magnatite Ingot. Right-clicked on a Mobile Core it is used up and binds the core to that player, who becomes its owner. A locked core admits its owner, their BSP-Core team and admins: only they may assemble, release, rename, buy a tier or mine it; everyone else may open the screen and read it. UNLOCK on the CORE tab (a second click within three seconds) gives the lock back; an admin's unlock drops it at the core. A mined locked core drops its lock.
- Stealing a locked core: an enemy within 3 blocks of the core clicks STEAL (a second click confirms). The steal runs 5 minutes, pauses while the thief is farther than 3 blocks, and fails when they stay away for 30 seconds, die or log out. The owner and their team are told in chat when it starts and ends; everyone with the screen open sees the red progress bar. At the end the thief owns the core, its construct and the lock. The core's screen shows a LOCKED chip with the owner's name, red while a steal runs. Settings in the server config under `lock`.
- A construct carrying a placed Shatter Totem is slowed and height-capped (server config `totem.maxSpeed`, 5 blocks a second, and `totem.maxHeight`, 64 blocks above the ground beneath); the Aboard box says so. The caps take effect once constructs drive.
- `com.mrgregles.bsp_mobile.api.MobilePositions` for other mods: where a block aboard a construct is drawn in the world (`docs/API.md`).
- GameTests: the lock binds and is used up, a locked core refuses strangers and admits its owner, unlocking, the steal's refusals, a steal that ends with the thief owning the core, a steal that fails when the thief stays away, the totem's caps, and the lock's recipe.
- The Mobile Core's tiers can be bought at the core. The UPGRADE tab shows the ladder with each step's price in Shatter Coins, the coins the viewer carries, and BUY (a second click within three seconds confirms). Whoever stands at the core may buy; the coins are taken from the buyer with change. The prices are in the server config (`tiers.prices`, one entry per step as `<coin tier>:<count>`; Tier II 6 Gold, Tier III 3 Diamond, Tier IV 2 Netherite, Tier V 1 Illyrium Shatter Coin by default).
- The Mobile Core is crafted from three parts, each with its own shaped recipe: the Mobile Core Processor (Tetrium Ingots, Redstone, a Diamond), the Mobile Core GPS (a Compass, Tetrium Ingots, Glass Panes) and the Mobile Core Gyroscope (Magnatite Ingots, Iron Bars, a Gold Ingot). The core takes the three parts with Iron Bars, a Tetrium Ingot and an Amethyst Block. The parts sit beside the core in the Functional Blocks tab.
- GameTests: a tier bought with change, a refusal when the buyer is short, the top tier, and the four recipes.

### Changed
- BSP-Core is a required mod (its Shatter Coins buy the tiers and its metals craft the parts). Valkyrien Skies and Kotlin for Forge remain required.
- The Mobile Core: a block (an iron cage round a glass heart, with a ring the client draws grey when not assembled, teal when parked and gold and turning when moving) that gathers every block touching the structure round it, through faces, edges and corners, up to its tier's allowance (200 blocks at Tier I; the ladder is in the server config), and turns them into a construct standing where they were built. Air and liquids are not gathered; bedrock and anything that cannot be mined, another Mobile Core, the blocks in the tag `bsp_mobile:never_mobile` (BSP-Core's admin blocks and Anti Totem blocks) and the config's list, and the blocks the engine lists as unmovable are refused with their reason. The core cannot be moved by pistons, is mined like an iron block, and keeps the construct's name and tier on its dropped item. While its construct is assembled it cannot be mined.
- The core's screen (right-click): CORE with the construct's name (typed on the screen), its state, the allowance with the tier, what was left out, what is aboard (totems, chests, machines, the owner), ASSEMBLE or RELEASE (a second click within three seconds confirms a release, which is refused while the construct moves) and SHOW OUTLINE; BLOCKS, the kinds of block the core takes with their counts, refused kinds first in red; UPGRADE, the tier ladder (buying comes later). While the screen is open the blocks the core takes are outlined in teal in the world and the refused ones in red.
- Releasing puts every block back into the world where the construct stands, turned to the nearest 90 degrees, block entities and their contents with them; nothing moves when a target cell is not free.
- The server config `serverconfig/bsp_mobile-server.toml`: the tier allowances, extra block ids a core never takes, and the speed above which a construct counts as moving.
- The project: a Forge 1.20.1 mod that requires Valkyrien Skies 2 (2.4.11 or newer) and Kotlin for Forge, with BSP-Core optional. One bridge class reaches Valkyrien Skies (assemble a block set into a construct, find the construct at a block, project a shipyard position into the world, remove a construct); nothing else in the mod sees the engine.
- GameTests: the mod loads beside Valkyrien Skies and BSP-Core, and eight blocks become a construct where they stood and can be taken back out.
