The Mobile Core: three block looks

Set A of the mock-ups, part 1. The Mobile Core is the one block a player crafts to make a base move (D3: it gathers every block touching the structure around it, up to its allowance). It has three states a player should read at a glance from across a base: not assembled (an ordinary block in the world), assembled and parked (the blocks round it are a construct, standing still) and moving. Each view shows the same look in those three states, left to right. Everything is drawn from shapes; the real block gets 16 x 16 textures from a generator script in the same colours. Pick one look, or name the parts to combine.

A1. The block

Look A: Keel Plate

A dark hull-plate cube with riveted corners, a light band running round its middle, and a gold compass rose set into the top. The band is the state: dark grey when not assembled, teal when parked, gold when moving. Reads as a piece of a ship's hull; sits flat in a floor or wall without drawing the eye until it lights.

Look B: Gyro Cage

An iron cage with each face open on a violet glass heart, and a teal gyroscope ring tilted inside it. The heart is the state: dim when not assembled, a steady teal ring when parked, the ring lit gold when moving. The most machine-like of the three; visible from every side, so it suits a core placed in the open on a deck.

Look C: Anchor Drum

A squat drum like a capstan on a square base plate, with chain links round the drum, hazard stripes on the base and one round lens on the front. The lens is the state: dark, teal, gold. Reads as deck machinery, something a vehicle would have; the stripes warn that this block is the thing that matters, as the totem's markings do.

Decisions for this set (defaults first)

  1. The block look. Default: Look A, Keel Plate. Or Look B, Gyro Cage. Or Look C, Anchor Drum. Or parts of two named together.
  2. The state colours. Default: dark grey when not assembled, teal when parked, gold when moving, matching the palette's use of teal for players' things and gold for value. Or a single teal light that only shows when assembled, with no moving state on the block itself.
  3. Its place in a build. Default: a full cube that can sit anywhere inside, on or under the structure (the gather starts from it in every direction). Or a block that must have open air on one face so its screen can be opened, like a chest.
