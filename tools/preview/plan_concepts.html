The PLAN tab: three layouts

D63 to D67: a flight plan of waypoints kept on the core, a cruise height, GO and STOP. The autopilot drives the helm the way a driver would, so every driving rule holds. Shown under way to waypoint 2 of 4 at a cruise height of 30. Default taken: Layout 1.

Layout 1. The list (default)

The waypoints as rows (number, position, distance), the next one highlighted and the passed ones dimmed; the cruise height box beside the header; keys: ADD HERE, REMOVE (the last), then GO or STOP.

Decisions taken (defaults, D63 to D67)

  1. Where the plan lives: on the core's PLAN tab, up to 16 waypoints. Or a Flight Plan block of its own.
  2. Making the plan: ADD HERE takes the viewer's position; REMOVE drops the last; the cruise height is typed. Or a map to click on.
  3. How it steers: it drives the helm's inputs like a driver, so every rule holds; a waypoint counts within 4 blocks; the last one parks the construct. Or direct forces that ignore the helm.
  4. Signals: a redstone pulse from the core on arrival; no helm, no plan. Or no signal.
  5. The tab: Layout 1. Or 2 or 3.
