The three parts of the Mobile Core: icon mock-ups

The Mobile Core Processor, the Mobile Core GPS and the Mobile Core Gyroscope (D22), the items the core is crafted from (D24). Three styles, each a set of three 16 x 16 icons drawn pixel by pixel, shown at 8x and then at inventory size beside BSP-Core's Tetrium Ingot, Magnatite Ingot and Basic Control Circuit so the family can be judged where it will live. The pick goes to tools/gen_core_assets.py, which draws the same pixel maps. Decisions at the end.

Style A. Plates

Each part is a riveted iron plate with the component set into it and a coloured strip along the foot

A uniform frame makes the three read as one kit at a glance: the same 14 x 14 bevelled plate, the emblem in the middle, and a strip in the part's colour (Tetrium violet for the Processor, teal for the GPS, gold for the Gyroscope). The Processor is a chip with gold pins and a teal die; the GPS a compass dial under glass with a red north needle and gold cardinal ticks; the Gyroscope two crossed Magnatite rings round a gold ball.

Style B. Objects

Each part is drawn as the thing itself, no frame, the way BSP-Core draws its ingots, circuits and motors

The biggest silhouettes of the three styles, so they read best at inventory size. The Processor is a square Tetrium chip with sixteen gold pins and a teal die on its trace square; the GPS a handheld unit with an antenna tipped red, a teal screen with a blip, two buttons and a gold key; the Gyroscope a free-standing Magnatite gimbal on an iron base with a gold ball at the pivot.

Style C. Core modules

Each part is a small cube held by four iron corner brackets, the Gyro Cage's own look, its heart in the part's colour

The parts look like pieces of the block they make: the cage's bracket corners round an 8 x 8 glass heart. The Processor's heart is Tetrium violet with a teal die and dark traces; the GPS's is deep teal with a pale dial ring and a red needle; the Gyroscope's is Magnatite blue with a gold ring round a bright pivot. Smallest emblems of the three, strongest family tie to the core.

The palette

Every colour is one already in the mod or in BSP-Core's items, so the parts sit beside them without a new hue: the core's iron greys and Tetrium violets, Magnatite's blue, the screen's teal and gold, redstone red for needles and tips, pale glass for dials. The letters are the pixel-map keys the generator uses.

Decisions (defaults first)

  1. 1, the style. Default: Style B, the parts drawn as objects, because they are the clearest at inventory size and match how BSP-Core draws its own parts. Or Style A, the three on matching iron plates with a coloured foot strip, if a uniform kit matters more. Or Style C, the bracketed cubes, if the tie to the Gyro Cage matters more.
  2. 2, the creative tab. Default: the three parts sit beside the Mobile Core in the Functional Blocks tab, so a player finds the whole kit in one place. Or in the Ingredients tab with vanilla's and BSP-Core's crafting parts.
  3. 3, the tooltip. Default: one grey line under each part's name, "A part of the Mobile Core." Or no tooltip line, the name alone.
  4. 4, the core's own icon. Default: the Mobile Core's item icon stays as it is (the cage over the heart with the teal cross, from MB-1). Or redrawn to match the chosen style of the parts.
