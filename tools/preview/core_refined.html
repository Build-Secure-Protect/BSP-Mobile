The Mobile Core, refined: the Gyro Cage and the tabbed screen

The picks of set A (D13 to D19) drawn the way they will be built. Points R1 to R5 at the end are what to confirm before code. Nothing here is in the mod yet.

R1. The block as a block model plus a renderer

Twelve cage bars and a glass heart from a model, a ring from a renderer

The cage is twelve bars 2 pixels thick along the cube's edges, a block model like any other, so it has a proper outline, lights and shadows and is cheap to draw. The heart is a 10-pixel translucent violet cube in the same model. The ring is not in the model: a block entity renderer draws it, so it can turn while the construct moves (one turn every two seconds) and stand still while parked; when not assembled it is dark grey and still. The heart glows full-bright whenever the construct exists. Left to right: not assembled, parked, moving.

not assembled: grey ring, dim heartparked: teal ring, lit heartmoving: gold ring, turning

In a build

The core set into a planks-and-stone hull as a player would place it (D15: anywhere, a full cube). Its open faces show the heart through the cage from every side, so a parked base shows a teal glow where its core is.

The faces at 16 x 16 (from the texture generator)

What tools/gen_core_assets.py will write. The cage face is the same on all six sides: bars at the edges with a lighter rivet pixel at each corner, open in the middle (the checker is transparency). The heart face is violet glass with a diagonal highlight; the renderer brightens it when lit. The item icon is the cage face over the heart face.

CORE

Points to confirm (defaults first)

  1. R1, how the block is drawn. Default: the cage and the heart are a block model, the ring comes from a renderer that turns it while moving and holds it still while parked, the heart glows full-bright whenever the construct exists. Or everything in the model, the ring fixed and never turning, no renderer.
  2. R2, what the block is like. Default: the same from every side (no facing); as hard to mine as an iron block; it drops itself and the item keeps the construct's name and tier; pistons cannot move it; a second core inside the gather is refused as "another Mobile Core". Or a lighter block, mined like planks, that pistons may push.
  3. R3, the textures. Default: the cage face, the heart face and the item icon come from tools/gen_core_assets.py as drawn above, in the palette's iron grey and violet. Or hand-drawn textures from the owner later, with the generated ones as stand-ins.
  4. R4, the CORE tab. Default: as drawn: name, state chip, allowance and tier, what was left out, the Aboard box, ASSEMBLE or RELEASE with SURE?, SHOW OUTLINE on by default. Or without the Aboard box, giving the allowance the whole width.
  5. R5, the gather's refusals. Default: bedrock, another Mobile Core, BSP-Core's admin blocks and Anti Totem blocks, and anything Valkyrien Skies lists as unmovable, each shown in red with its reason. Or only bedrock and other cores refused, everything else taken.
