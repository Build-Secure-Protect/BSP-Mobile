The Mobile Helm: block looks and the HELM tab

D5 and D6: a helm anyone can reach drives the construct on land and water (flying later); a locked core keeps the helm for those it admits. Three looks for the helm block, each empty and with a driver at it; three layouts for the core's HELM tab. Defaults taken under the blanket go: Look A and Layout 1, open to change on the testing pass.

1. The helm block: three looks

Look A. Helm Wheel (default)

A waist-high riveted iron pedestal with a ship's wheel rimmed in Tetrium violet, facing one way. The driver stands at it, held in place like a seat, looking where it faces; W and S thrust, A and D turn, sneak steps away. The wheel turns slightly with the input.

Look B. Console

A lectern-shaped iron console with a sloped top, a gold lever and a teal dial that lights while driving. The driver stands behind it. Reads as machinery, fits a base more than a ship.

Look C. Captain's Seat

An iron chair with a violet cushion and two stub levers at the armrests. The driver sits. The most legible "someone is driving" from a distance; the least room-friendly.

2. The HELM tab on the core's screen

Shown with a driver at the helm and the construct under way at 6.2 blocks a second, heading south-west. The ANCHOR key is for those the lock admits (everyone on an unlocked core).

Layout 1. Readouts (default)

Three readouts in a row (speed, heading, driver), the helm count and the anchor state below, and one key: DROP ANCHOR while free, WEIGH ANCHOR while parked. Nothing moves on the screen; the numbers refresh with the view.

Decisions taken (defaults, D36 to D43)

  1. The helm block: Look A, the Helm Wheel. Or B the Console. Or C the Captain's Seat.
  2. The controls: W and S thrust along the helm's facing, A and D turn, sprint doubles the thrust, sneak leaves; no input lets drag stop the construct. Or thrust only, with the turn from the driver's look direction.
  3. The figures: acceleration 4 blocks/s², top speed 8 blocks/s, turn 30°/s, drag that halves the speed in about 2 s, all in the config; a totem aboard caps the top speed. Or slower, 5 blocks/s top, for a base that creeps.
  4. Floating: a buoyancy factor (4.0) so a hull of ordinary blocks floats like a boat. Or the engine's natural buoyancy, where most hulls sink.
  5. Parking: the helm unparks; 3 s after stopping with nobody at a helm the construct parks itself; ANCHOR parks or frees it by hand. Or never parks itself.
  6. Who may drive: anyone, unless the core is locked, when the lock decides. Or the owner and team always.
  7. The HELM tab: Layout 1, the readouts. Or 2 the compass rose. Or 3 the gauge strip.
  8. The recipe: Iron Ingots round a Compass with a Tetrium Ingot on top. Or with a GPS part instead of the compass.
