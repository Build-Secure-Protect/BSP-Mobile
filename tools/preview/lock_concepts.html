The Mobile Core Lock: icon and screen mock-ups

D23: a lock upgrade binds a Mobile Core to its owner, and an enemy takes a locked core by a timed steal in the order of a totem steal, after which the core and its construct are theirs. Three icon styles for the lock item (in the Style B family of the parts), then three ways the CORE tab can show a lock and a running steal. Sample names and times; the decisions are at the end.

1. The lock item: three icons

Three objects, drawn the way the three parts are

Padlock: an iron shackle over a Tetrium-violet body with a teal keyhole, the plainest reading of "lock". Clamp: two Magnatite brackets closed on a gold bar with a teal light, a thing that bolts onto the core. Seal: a violet disc with the core's teal cross and four iron rivets, a seal pressed onto the core.

2. The CORE tab with a lock on, as the thief sees it mid-steal

Every layout keeps MB-1's CORE tab (name, state, allowance, Aboard, the keys) and adds the lock. Shown: an enemy named Rook stealing "Harbour Lantern" from its owner Mrgregles, 2 minutes 31 seconds of 5 left. The owner sees the same, with UNLOCK where STEAL is. A player who is neither sees the lock and no key for it.

Layout A. A chip and a key

The lightest touch: a violet LOCKED chip beside the state chip naming the owner; STEAL joins the key row (SURE? on the second click); while a steal runs a thin red bar sits under the keys with the thief's name and the time left, and the LOCKED chip turns red. Everything else stays where it was.

Decisions (defaults first)

  1. 1, the scope of MB-3. Default: MB-3 builds the core lock (the item, binding, the timed steal, the screen) and the totem rule's data (the caps a construct with a totem aboard will obey, stored for the driving task) with the request rows to BSP-Core; the helm lock arrives with the helm in MB-4 and reuses the same lock. Or MB-3 waits until the helm exists and builds both locks then.
  2. 2, the lock item. Default: "Mobile Core Lock", shaped in the crafting table: 4 Tetrium Ingots at the edges, 4 Iron Ingots at the corners and a Magnatite Ingot in the middle, its icon from this page. Or a dearer lock with a Netherite Ingot in the middle, so locking is a late-game act.
  3. 3, the icon. Default: the Padlock. Or the Clamp. Or the Seal.
  4. 4, putting the lock on and taking it off. Default: right-click the core with the lock in hand: the lock is used up and the core binds to that player, who becomes its owner; the owner takes it off with UNLOCK on the CORE tab (a second click within three seconds) and gets the lock item back. Or the lock sits in a slot on the UPGRADE tab and is taken out from there.
  5. 5, who a lock admits. Default: the owner and their teammates (BSP-Core's Teams) may ASSEMBLE, RELEASE, RENAME, BUY a tier and, later, drive; everyone else may open the screen and read it. The owner's totem friends are admitted as soon as BSP-Core offers a call for it (a request row). Or the owner alone.
  6. 6, the steal. Default: an enemy within 3 blocks of a locked core clicks STEAL (a second click within three seconds); the steal runs 5 minutes (config), pauses while the thief is farther than 3 blocks, and fails when they stay away for 30 seconds, die or log out; the owner and their teammates get a chat warning when it starts and when it ends, and everyone with the screen open sees the progress; at the end the thief owns the core, the construct and the lock, which stays on. One steal at a time per core; the distance is measured in the construct's own space, so a steal works aboard a moving construct. Or the thief must hold right-click on the core for the whole time, as if mining it.
  7. 7, the screen. Default: Layout A, the chip and the key. Or Layout B, the lock box. Or Layout C, the banner.
  8. 8, admins. Default: BSP-Core admins act on any locked core without stealing it, and may UNLOCK it (the lock item drops at the core). Or admins obey locks like anyone else.
  9. 9, the totem aboard. Default: a construct carrying a placed Shatter Totem moves at most 5 blocks a second and no higher than 64 blocks above the ground beneath it (both config); MB-3 stores these caps and shows "Totem aboard: slowed and height-capped" in the Aboard box; the driving task enforces them. Or leave the numbers until the driving task.
  10. 10, the requests to BSP-Core. Default: two rows now: steals and every other totem distance check project a shipyard position into the world through a hook BSP-Mobile provides by class name; and a call that says whether a player is on a totem owner's friends list, for the lock. Or only the first row now.
